# EXPLORATION — ten sections, ten rooms

Round 20, branch `design/voices`. The client's verdict on round 19 was exact:
the slab died but the *armature* survived — claim top-left, chapter rail
top-right, one rule at the same y, hero figure lower-left, mono block right —
and ten variations on one frontispiece are still one frontispiece.

This pass inverts the coherence model. **Shared material** (the mono face, the
serif bracket, the two palettes, one-hue-per-system, the refusal to decorate,
the claims machinery) — **unshared armature**. Each section gets its own
compositional idea, derived from that section's argument, plus its own motion
verb. The test: identify any section from a 200px crop with the words blurred.

For each section: the argument in one line, at least three directions
considered, and the choice with the reason.

---

## 0 · Thesis — *one maker, six systems, an index to the page*

Directions considered:

- **A. The orbital.** Six systems orbiting the name, hue-coded, radial spokes.
  Rejected: decorative — the orbit implies a relationship (periodicity?
  gravity?) that doesn't exist, and radial is spent better on AutoML, where
  the geometry *is* the claim.
- **B. The exploded shelf.** Six miniature isometric plates in an axonometric
  stack, like a plate book seen from its fore-edge. Rejected: miniatures of
  plates that don't exist yet at 60u wide say nothing; it reads as chrome.
- **C. The title page and index.** A book's opening: centered axis (the only
  centered composition until the colophon answers it), the serif title, then
  a table of plates with dot leaders running to Roman numerals — each system's
  mark and hue taught in its own index row. Chosen: the page IS a plate book;
  its opening should behave like one, and a leadered index is unmistakable in
  any crop. Nothing else on the page is centered or symmetric, so the two
  bookend plates (0 and IX) carry the serif bracket *spatially*, not just
  typographically.

**Chosen: C.** Motion: none — a title page that fidgets is a banner ad
(round 19's finding, kept). Entry point: dead center.

## I · Work — *a year of paid work; the warrant is testimony, not a repo*

- **A. The timeline.** Jun 2025–May 2026 as a horizontal bar with event marks.
  Rejected: a timeline argues sequence; nothing here is about sequence.
- **B. The affidavit.** A prose-like document with a signature line. Rejected:
  the section's numbers are its spine, and prose-shaped SVG duplicates the
  README under the plate.
- **C. The ledger.** An account book: date column at the left, description in
  the middle, **amounts right-aligned at the page edge** — the only plate
  whose numbers live at the right margin — with full-width ruled lines, a
  double rule opening and closing the account, and a rotated hollow **ATTESTED**
  stamp struck over the closing. The stamp is the section's entire
  epistemology (vouched, not derived) made into the plate's identity.
  Chosen: a ledger is what a year of paid work *is*, and the stamp answers
  "why should I believe this" on the plate's face.

**Chosen: C.** Motion verb: **stamp** — once per loop the attestation lands
(scale-settle, then still ~93% of the loop). The one gesture is the warrant.
Entry point: the right-hand amount column.

## II · Glyph — *I don't trust the library, so the net is handwritten — and here are all 299 errors*

- **A. The specimen drawer.** The 299 as a museum grid under glass, hero
  numbers beside. That is round 19's plate — the client already called the
  family it belongs to boring.
- **B. Figure-ground inversion.** The 299 as a full-bleed field with the
  score knocked out of its middle. Strong crop identity, but the clearing
  fights check 3 (text inside a field of glyphs) and the field stops reading
  as *evidence* — it becomes texture, which is the one thing the errors must
  never be.
- **C. The copybook.** A handwriting practice sheet: full-bleed ruled
  baselines edge to edge, an amber margin line, and the 299 true labels
  *sitting on the rules* — the machine's failed handwriting homework, graded.
  The hero 299 is drawn by the pen (kept — the claim is "by hand", and the
  gesture is the claim); the 79 confident errors stay heavy-stroke. Chosen:
  the net is *learning to read handwriting*; its errors belong on a writing
  exercise sheet, and edge-to-edge rules + a margin line are identifiable in
  a 40px crop, let alone 200.

**Chosen: C.** Motion verb: **draw** (the pen, kept from round 19 — it was
never the problem). Entry: top-left hero, then the ruled field owns the plate.
The chapter mark becomes a rotated binder tab on the left edge — the first
plate whose numbering is not horizontal.

## III · jetpack — *I don't trust my own optimisation; print the reference I lose to*

- **A. The log-scale race.** All five throughputs on one log axis. Rejected
  after drawing it on paper: gzip throughput and checksum throughput are
  different workloads, and one shared axis visually invites exactly the
  cross-task comparison the prose is careful never to make. A dishonest
  chart with honest numbers is still dishonest.
- **B. The oscilloscope.** Throughput as traces on a scope grid. Rejected:
  a scope draws *time series*; these are point estimates with intervals. Form
  contradicting data.
- **C. The bench sheet.** A test-bench record: two measured lanes, each with
  its own axis baseline and proportional solid bars — COMPRESS (66.2 vs 422,
  the 6.4×) and CHECKSUM (1.52 / 4.26 / **14.06**) — where the JDK intrinsic's
  bar is drawn longest and tipped "not beaten"; the checksum-equality readout
  beside it; the bounded in-flight window kept as the one mechanism drawn
  working. Chosen: the section's argument is *measurement over pride*, and a
  bench sheet where the reference bar visibly outruns yours is that argument
  with no caption needed.

**Chosen: C.** Motion verb: **rotate** — the in-flight window cycles its four
blocks (kept: the bound shown binding is the system's one unusual mechanism).
The bars hold still; a benchmark that wiggles is a benchmark you doubt.
Chapter mark: an instrument badge, boxed, bottom-right.

## IV · Cadence — *every parsed span carries the parser that produced it*

- **A. The pipeline.** Sentence in, four parser boxes, calendar out. Rejected:
  boxes-and-arrows is the genre this page is trying to leave.
- **B. The interlinear gloss.** The sentence with a second line of
  parser names under each word, linguistics-style. Close — but a gloss labels
  *every* word, and the plate's sharpest fact is that the title carries **no**
  label. A form that demands total annotation can't draw an absence.
- **C. The marked-up manuscript.** The sentence as a large specimen, and the
  spans annotated the way an editor marks a proof: underline, then an elbow
  leader out to a margin note naming the parser — `compromise` left,
  `chrono-node` right — while the title stays conspicuously unmarked. The
  chrono leader continues on and *files* the chip into the FRI 1pm slot, so
  parsing and filing are one continuous drawn line. Chosen: proof-correction
  marks are precisely "provenance written next to the text", which is the
  system's whole claim; and callout elbows drawn outward from a sentence are
  like nothing else on the page.

**Chosen: C.** Motion verb: **annotate** — the marks and leaders draw
themselves, then the chip lands (one clock, one compound gesture).
Entry: the sentence itself — the only plate whose hero is an input, in quotes.

## V · Applied — *cheapest first, and allowed to say "I don't know"*

- **A. The three-layer stack.** Round 19's form. Already rebuilt once; still
  a stack of labeled boxes at the right of a hero number — the template.
- **B. The decision tree.** Branches per outcome. Rejected: the cascade is
  not a tree, it is a *fall* — order matters, cost rises downward.
- **C. The sieve in section.** A vertical mineral-sort: the plate's spine is
  a funnel drawn in cross-section, three screens of narrowing width labeled
  inside, envelopes leaving sideways at each screen (cheap layers catch
  most), the 0.85 confidence line drawn as a physical gate bar near the
  bottom — and the one message that fails it is *walked sideways to a human*,
  who is on the plate. Verdict block at the bottom. Chosen: the argument is
  literally "cheapest first, decline below a threshold", which is a sorting
  screen's job description; and it makes Applied the page's one **vertical**
  composition — centered spine, annotations alternating sides, read top to
  bottom like a core sample.

**Chosen: C.** Motion verb: **fall** (and be caught): one message descends the
sieve, is held at the gate — the pause is the refusal — and is diverted to
the human. Entry: top center, then straight down.

## VI · The refusal — *the app forgot to filter; the database refused*

- **A. The wall in plan.** The query meets RLS drawn as a wall. Rejected in
  round 18 already: "token meets line" was the page's most-repeated gesture.
- **B. The court record.** Transcript typography, Q&A. Rejected: the database
  does not argue, it declines — silence is the point, dialogue is the wrong
  form.
- **C. The redacted disclosure.** A FOIA response: the audit table keeps its
  named services and guard marks, but tenant A's column comes back as
  **redaction bars** — blacked out by row-level security, not by the app —
  while B's rows return intact. The unfiltered query is printed above like a
  records request; the response, at the largest type on the whole page, is
  the word **B only**. Chosen: a redacted document is what this system
  actually *produces* — you asked for everything and received what you were
  entitled to — and six black bars on a page are the strongest 200px
  identity in the whole set. The plate stays the broadside, bottom-weighted,
  the only composition whose mass sits at its foot.

**Chosen: C.** Motion verb: **redact** — once per loop the six bars wipe
across A's column in a 150ms wave (round 19's invisible 2u strikes replaced
by 16u bars a raster gate can actually see). The two caveat footnotes stay:
an audit that means it keeps its asterisks.

## VII · LifeQuest — *routines recur; one source tree is built twice; the generator declines to invent*

- **A. The quest board.** Cards on a board. Rejected: cards. Enough said.
- **B. The rotation list.** Round 19's cycling checklist — good motion,
  but formally it is the same left-column-list as everything else.
- **C. The circle line.** A transit map: the four routines are stations on a
  loop line (a routine is a thing you come around to again), one rider
  travels the loop continuously, the line's junction forks to two termini —
  the Tauri binary and the web build, one tree leaving down two branches —
  and a generation spur runs OPENAI → HUGGING FACE → RETURNS NOTHING, its
  last station hollow: a terminus where no train is invented. Chosen: the
  map is the argument three times over (recurrence = the loop, the build fork
  = a junction, the declining generator = a dead spur), it is warm in a way
  the one consumer product on the page has earned, and it is the first use
  of motion-along-a-path in the document (probed: `offset-path` is
  enumerable, seekable, and bbox-visible to every gate).

**Chosen: C.** Motion verb: **ride** — continuous, slow, the only unbroken
motion on the page, because a routine does not arrive, it goes around.
Chapter mark: a route badge — VII in a filled pink roundel.

## VIII · Agentic AutoML — *the model holds 15 tools; the other 29 arrive with the phase*

- **A. The registry strip.** Round 19's 44-tick strip with the stepping
  cluster. Honest, but it is a ruler — one more horizontal figure.
- **B. The exploded container.** Lead with the sandbox as an isometric
  exploded assembly. Strong, but the sandbox is the *second* claim; the
  tool-scoping is the unusual one and deserves the composition.
- **C. The phase dial.** Seven phases as sectors of a dial; the model's 15
  carried tools live in a hub that never moves (they travel with the model —
  so they are simply *always there*); the 29 phase tools sit as ticks around
  the rim, each lighting only while its sector is active; a needle steps
  phase to phase, and the needle is the routing. The sandbox becomes a small
  isometric vessel below — read-only hull, five tmpfs trays exploded out on
  leaders, the only writable surface lifted out for inspection. Chosen: the
  claim "one phase, one tool set" is a *rotary* fact — position selects
  capability, like a microscope turret — and the page has no radial
  composition anywhere else.

**Chosen: C.** Motion verb: **advance** — the needle steps through seven
sectors, sector and ticks lighting under it; four devices on one clock, as
round 19 required, now arranged on a circle. Entry: the hub.

## IX · Colophon — *if a number here is wrong, it is wrong in public*

- **A. The spec block.** Machine-set list of mechanisms. Rejected: the
  colophon is the author's voice, not the machine's.
- **B. The last ledger row.** Close the work plate's account. Rejected:
  the colophon answers the *thesis*, not §I; the bracket must close where it
  opened.
- **C. The imprint.** A printer's colophon: centered like the title page it
  answers, the serif speaking last, the six product marks set as a small
  printer's device, the mechanisms in one quiet centered line. Chosen: the
  two centered plates bracket eight asymmetric ones — the serif's bracket
  made spatial — and an imprint is the truthful genre: this is where a book
  states how it was made and who answers for it.

**Chosen: C.** Motion: none. The page ends at rest.

## What holds the ten rooms together

One mono face and its one serif counterpoint; the same two grounds; the same
six hues meaning the same six systems (taught in the index, plate 0);
hairline rules at 1u everywhere structure is drawn; every number still
re-derived (53 claims, zero new unregistered numbers — the two compositions
that wanted new axis numbers were redesigned until they didn't need them);
every honesty device kept and two promoted (ATTESTED became a stamp, the
refusal's absence became redaction). The armature is gone; the material is
the coherence.

## What the gates had to learn

Checks 5 and 12 in gate.mjs were the armature's enforcement arm: one fixed
type column for every plate, and first-ink/right-edge/bottom-margin equality
*across* plates — a machine that fails any page whose sections differ. Both
re-authored (reasoning in the file): each plate now **declares** its frame
(`data-col`, `data-frame`) and the gate holds the render to the declaration —
edges remain decisions with a machine behind them; they are no longer the
same decision ten times.
